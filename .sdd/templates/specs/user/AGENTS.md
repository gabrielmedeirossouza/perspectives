# User

## Feature

A screen or flow owns visible behavior independently across every channel delivering it. Web, mobile, and CLI are channels, not feature boundaries.

## Scope

| Holds | Leaves out |
|-------|------------|
| Routes, interactions, flow states, UI copy, visible layout | Business who, when, and limits; UI stacks, component conventions, design tokens, and accessibility mechanisms |

## Vocabulary

| Write | Leave out |
|-------|-----------|
| English surface prose: `empty list`, `confirm deletion` | Technical names, business policy |
