# Operator

## Feature

An operational or delivery-lifecycle boundary owns policy independently, such as promotion, rollback, or incident response.

## Scope

| Holds | Leaves out |
|-------|------------|
| Who may ship, promotion gates, rollback windows, incident obligations | Product and screen behavior; pipelines, images, runtime, and observability mechanisms |

## Vocabulary

| Write | Leave out |
|-------|-----------|
| English operations prose: `production environment`, `rollback`, `promotion` | Tool names, file paths, commands |
