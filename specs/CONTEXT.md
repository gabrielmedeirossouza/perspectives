# Glossary

Canonical vocabulary of this tree. Terms here define what things are — behavior and rules live in spec files.

## Access

**Member**:
Person with an account in Ledger.
_Avoid_: user, customer

**Account**:
The identity of one member: an email address, proven by a password.

**Session**:
The period in which a member is signed in, from sign-in to sign-out or expiry.
_Avoid_: login
